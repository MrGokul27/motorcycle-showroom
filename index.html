Showroom FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Buying & Test Rides

  • What documents do I need for a showroom test ride?

    Please bring a valid full UK Category A motorcycle driving licence (held for a minimum of 12 months), a DVLA check code generated within the last 21 days, and proof of address. Full CE-approved riding gear is available in our boutique if needed.

  • Do you offer nationwide enclosed motorcycle delivery across the UK?

    Yes! Every motorcycle purchased from our 245 Great Portland Street, London flagship can be delivered anywhere in England, Scotland, or Wales inside our climate-controlled, air-ride suspended Stackly transporter van.

  • Can I part-exchange my current motorcycle or car against a new superbike?

    Absolutely. We accept both motorcycles and prestige cars in part-exchange. We also settle any existing finance agreements directly and apply your equity toward your new PCP or HP deposit.

  • What is covered under your 50-Point Factory Master Inspection?

    Our technicians perform ultrasonic brake caliper servicing, Dynojet compression and AFR verification, ECU software updates, fork oil renewal, and laser frame/wheel alignment before issuing a 24-month warranty certificate.
