Workshop Wisdom

Quarterly Maintenance Habits From Our Master Mechanics

Every 500 Miles
525 / 520 X-Ring Chain Care

Clean with kerosene-safe degreaser, verify 28–30mm slack on side stand, and apply ceramic wax lube to inner rollers while warm.

Before Every Ride
Cold Tyre Pressure Check

Even a 3 PSI drop alters steering geometry. Check cold pressures (typically 36F / 42R road, 31F / 26R track) with a calibrated gauge.

Every 12 Months
Hygroscopic Brake Fluid Flush

DOT 4 / RBF660 fluid absorbs moisture over time. Annual bleeding preserves crisp Brembo radial master cylinder lever feel.

Tailored to Rider
Static & Rider Suspension Sag

Set 30–35mm front and 28–32mm rear rider sag wearing full leather gear so your electronic damping operates in its sweet spot.

Reader Mailbag

Top Technical Questions Answered by Our Workshop

  • Does fitting a full titanium exhaust void my showroom warranty?

    When supplied and fitted by our authorized master technicians at Stackly Motorcycle Showroom using factory-approved Akrapovic or Termignoni up-maps, your 24-month showroom warranty remains 100% intact.

  • Should I choose E5 Super Unleaded (99 RON) for my superbike?

    Yes. High-compression (14.0:1+) V4 and inline-four engines perform best on 98–99 RON E5 Super Unleaded, preventing knock-sensor timing retardation and protecting fuel system elastomers during storage.

  • How often should electronic semi-active fork oil be replaced?

    We recommend refreshing Ohlins and WP semi-active fork oil every 12,000 road miles or after every 6 circuit track days to keep stepper-motor needle valves clean and responsive.
