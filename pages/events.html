Track & Event Rules

Event Participation & Safety Questions

  • What riding gear is mandatory for Stackly circuit track days?

    All UK circuit venues require a one-piece or 360-degree zip-together two-piece leather suit, ACU Gold-stamped full-face helmet, CE Level 2 back protector, gauntlet leather gloves, and ankle-braced racing boots.

  • What is the exhaust noise limit at Silverstone and Donington Park?

    Our noisy track days allow up to 105 dB static / 102 dB drive-by. Our showroom technicians bring removable dB killers and acoustic baffles to the paddock free of charge for all Stackly riders.

  • Can Stackly transport my motorcycle from London to the circuit?

    Yes! Drop your motorcycle at 245 Great Portland Street, London 48 hours before the event and our enclosed race transporter will deliver it directly to your heated pit box.
